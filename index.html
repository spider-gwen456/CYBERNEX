01 / WHO WE ARE

Understanding the Digital Shield.

CYBERNEX is a cybersecurity awareness platform designed to make digital security easier to understand.

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems, and sensitive information from unauthorized access and cyber attacks.

Our goal is to encourage smarter digital habits and help people navigate the online world safely.

02 / KNOW THE RISKS

Common Cyber Threats.

Understanding common cyber threats is the first step towards building a safer digital environment.

🎣

Phishing

Fraudulent messages or websites designed to trick people into revealing personal or sensitive information.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software created to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to computer systems.

🔒

Ransomware

A type of malware that locks or encrypts data and demands payment to restore access.

🎭

Social Engineering

Psychological manipulation used to trick individuals into sharing confidential information or taking unsafe actions.

03 / STAY PROTECTED

Build Better Security Habits.

Small security practices can make a big difference in protecting your digital identity.

01

Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords using a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols.

02

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra verification step to your accounts for improved protection.

03

Avoid Suspicious Links

Verify unexpected messages and links before clicking or sharing information.

04

Keep Software Updated

Install security updates to fix vulnerabilities and improve protection.
